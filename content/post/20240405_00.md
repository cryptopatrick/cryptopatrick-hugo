# SAP : Systems Applications Products in Digital Processing
SAP was founded back in 1972, when five german IBM employees were informed that the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software project that they had been working on, was being scrapped. Presumeably, the group of five rage-quit and started their own software company - SAP.

Among the original co-founders; Hasso Plattner, Klaus Tschira, Claus Wellenreuther, Dietmar Hopp, and Hans-Werner Hector - only Mr. Plattner is still active in SAP, where he acts as a Chairman of th SAP Advisory Board.

The current CEO of SAP is Bill McDermott.
