# Linear Algebra
An _algebra_ consists of _objects_ and _rules_ for manipulating them.
In Linear Algebra, the objects are _vectors_ and the rules cover the manipulation 
of their position, and the addition and multiplication with each other.

The _addition_ and _scalar multiplication_ of vectors produce new vectors.
The set of all vectors that can be created with the rules is called a _vector set_.
A major idea is that of _closure_: will the addition or scalar multiplication of
two vectors create a vector that is part of the original set?
