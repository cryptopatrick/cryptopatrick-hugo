In a [tweet](https://x.com/ylecun/status/1640122342570336267) from 2023, Meta Ai researcher and CNN inventor, Yann LeCun conjectures that
"Auto-Regressive LLMs are doomed". These are notes taken while trying to understand
his argument.

## Auto-Regressive LLMs (AR-LLMs)
Claim: Auto-Regressive LLMs are _exponentially diverging diffusion processes_.
Okay, let's approach this by starting with some definitions:  
+ diffusion process
+ exponentially diverging
+ auto-regressive
+ LLM

## Yann LeCun's argument
We start by letting $e$ be the _probability_ that any _generated token_ __exits__ 
the tree of "correct" answers.

+ $e$: probability that any produced token takes us outside the set of correct answers.
+ $\mathcal{P}(\text{correct})$: probability that answers of length $n$ is is correct.


```rust
// Let's create a model that can generate a probability distrubution.
// and then use it to generate longer and longer n-grams.
```


Then the probability that an _answer_ of length $n$ is _correct_ is $(1-e)^n$
$$\mathcal{P}(\text{correct}) = (1-e)^n$$

LeCun claims that AR-LLMs are _doomed_ and that they cannot made factual, non-toxic,
controllable, and the problem is not fixable without a major redesign.

One can mitigate the problem by making e smaller (through training) but one simply cannot eliminate the problem entirely.
A solution would require to make LLMs non auto-regressive while preserving their fluency.
