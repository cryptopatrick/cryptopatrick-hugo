  ## Theorem 1

  Let players $P_1$, $P_2$, and $P_3$ fire with probabilities `x`, `y`, and `z` respectively, 
  with `x > y > z`. The best strategy for all players is:
  1 _aims_ at 2, 2 aims at 1, and 3 aims at 1.

  Sequential Truel

  2.  The probability that any $P$ hits her target is fixed: for $P_1$, the chance of 
  3.  All combatants are aware of others combatants abilities (probability of hitting a target)
  4.  There is no abstaining (missing on purpose, firing into the air, not firing)
  5.  Each combatant has an unlimited supply of ammunition
  6.  A player can only pick one strategy for the entire truel.
  7.  If a combatant is eliminated, then the remaining two combatants engage in a duel, 
      until a single combantant remain.


  ## Strategy
  Once combatants are in position, they need to determine _if there exists_ a __best__
  strategy, and if so what that strategy is.

  At the start of a truel, each combatant has two targets to select from.
  This gives a eight possible strategies.
  There's an assumption that each combatant is picking the strategy that is belived
  to maximise its chances of survial.

  ## Theorem 1 Destroy the Stronger Opponent Strategy
  Basically, in this strategy, each combatant targets the opponent with the greatest
  probability of hitting its target.



  $$
  \mathsf{\text{Let combatants }}\mathsf{C_1, C_2, C_3} \mathsf{\text{ fire with probabilities }}
  p, q, r \mathsf{\text{ respectively, where }} p>q>r. \\\
  \mathsf{\text{The best strategy for all combatants is that: }} \\\
  C_1 \mathsf{\text{ fires on }} C_2 \\\
  C_2 \mathsf{\text{ fires on }} C_1 \\\
  C_3 \mathsf{\text{ fires on }} C_1 \\\
  $$
