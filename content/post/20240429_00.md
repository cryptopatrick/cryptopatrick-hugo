# Begin
If we really want to understand LLMs on a mathematical level, 
a good place to start is to understand _word embeddings_. 
The following paper, from 2014, is a good jump-off-point:
> [word2vec](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.3781)

# Next
After that, check out the following paper from 2017:
> ["Attention is All You Need"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

# Finally
Then read the following papers in order:
1. the [LoRA paper 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)
2. and [the Chain-of-thought paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.11903) 2022/2023
3. the [Toolformers paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761) 2023

These are the technical foundations for most LLM research going on at the moment.
