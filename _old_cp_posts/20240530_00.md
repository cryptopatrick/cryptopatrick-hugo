## Complex Analysis
In complex analysis, functions of complex variables are studied, leading to profound results like the __Cauchy-Riemann equations__, __Cauchy's integral theorem__, and __residue theory__, which are essential in evaluating _integrals_ and _series_.

## Definition
A _complex number_ is an expression of the form $a + ib$, where $a$ and $b \in R$, and 
$i$ is just a symbol. The set of all complex numbers are expressed as $\mathbb{C}$.
For a complex number $z=a + ib$, $Re(z) = a$ is the _real part_ of $z$, and 
$Im(z)=b$ is the _imaginary part_ of $z$.

## Basic Operations over $\mathbb{C}$
+ Addition: $(a + bi) + (c + di) = (a+c)+(b+d)i$.
+ Subtraction: $(a + bi) - (c + di) = (a-c)+(b-d)i$.
+ Multiplication: $(a + bi)(c + di) = (ac-bd)+(bc+ad)i$.
