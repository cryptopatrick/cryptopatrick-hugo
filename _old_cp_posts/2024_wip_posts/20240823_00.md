__Definition (Game):__ A contest between one or more opposing players with 
certain specified rules.

__Definition (Duel):__ A *game* between two opposing players, where the goal
for each player is to eliminate the other player.

__Definition (Sequential Duel):__ A *duel*  with the following rules:  
1. Players fire in sequence. First player 1, then player 2, then player 1, and so on.
2. The probability that any player hits his target is fixed.
For player 1 the probability is expressed as $p$.
For player 2 the probability is expressed as $q$.
3. There is no abstaining (no passing, no missing on purpose). 
It is assumed that each opponent does his utmost to eliminate his opponent.
4. We're assuming that each player has unlimited supply of ammunition.
