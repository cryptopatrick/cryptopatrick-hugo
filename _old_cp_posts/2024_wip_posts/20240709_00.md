Infantry radio operators in the U.S. Army use a standardized set of procedures and terminology when calling in artillery support.

This language is structured and follows established protocols to ensure clarity, precision, and effectiveness. 

The most commonly used format for requesting artillery fire is known as the Call for Fire.
