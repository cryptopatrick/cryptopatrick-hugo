Grassmann Algebra is named after its discoveror, Hermann Gunther Grassmann.
In Grassmann Algebra, addition, negation, and multiplication by a scalar value
carries the same meaning as in 'normal' Vector and Linear Algebra.
It's the product operation that is different.
